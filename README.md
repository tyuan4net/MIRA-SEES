# MIRA-SEES

## MIRA Spacetime Expression and Exchange Standard

**A shared spacetime language for people, AI, and software.**

MIRA-SEES makes birth and event spacetime information easier to write by hand, discuss, exchange, validate, and compute. It distinguishes civil time, UTC, local mean time, local solar time, and different kinds of geographic information without discarding the meaning of the original record.

One normalized birth record can become the shared anchor for structured outputs from Human Design, Western and classical astrology, Hindu astrology and Jyotish, Bazi, Zi Wei Dou Shu, Cayce Astrology, Jungian Astrology, and other systems. Those outputs can later be connected with life events, observations, tags, and relationships, then interpreted by people, AI, or independently written software.

MIRA-SEES does not merge these traditions into one rule set. It gives them a common, traceable way to say **which time, which place, and which transformation** their calculations used.

```text
Birth or event record
        ↓
MIRA-SEES normalization
        ↓
Structured multi-system data
        ↓
Life events and observations
        ↓
Human, AI, and third-party interpretation
```

## MIRA 時空表達與交換標準

**一套讓人、AI 與程式共同使用的時空語言。**

MIRA-SEES 讓出生及事件的時空資訊更容易被手寫、討論、交換、驗證與計算。它區分行政時間、UTC、地方平太陽時、地方真太陽時，以及不同種類的地理資訊，同時保留原始紀錄的語意。

一組正規化的出生資料，可以成為 Human Design、西洋與古典占星、印度占星／Jyotish、八字、紫微斗數、凱西占星、榮格占星及其他系統共用的時空錨點。各系統產生的結構化資料，未來可以與人生事件、觀察、標籤及關係連結，再由人、AI 或其他人撰寫的程式進行解讀。

MIRA-SEES 不把不同傳統混成同一套規則。它提供共同而可追溯的方式，說清楚計算使用了**哪一種時間、哪一個地點，以及哪一條轉換路徑**。

```text
出生或事件紀錄
      ↓
MIRA-SEES 正規化
      ↓
跨系統結構化資料
      ↓
人生事件與觀察
      ↓
人、AI 與第三方程式解讀
```
